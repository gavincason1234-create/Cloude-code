Before you power it

3.3 V, and its own regulator

Supply range is 1.9–3.6 V. The LNA is active in receive and draws far more than a bare module, so feed it from an AMS1117-3.3 off the 5 V pin, not the board's 3V3. Signal lines are 5 V tolerant — no level shifter needed.

100 µF at the module

Fit the cap across VCC and GND at the module pins, not at the regulator. Missing decoupling is the most common cause of "it reads all zeros" — a browning-out module still answers SPI and simply reports a quiet band.

Set the LNA gain in firmware

The RPD trips near −64 dBm at the chip, but the LNA sits ahead of it, so the real floor is about −84 dBm. Set AEGIS_NRF_LNA_GAIN_DB to 20 here, 0 for a bare module.
